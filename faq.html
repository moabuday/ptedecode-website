Choosing your exam

What's the difference between PTE Academic and PTE Core?
PTE AcademicPTE Core
Best forUniversity and college study, professional registration, and work and migration visas in many countriesCanadian immigration: permanent residency and citizenship (accepted by IRCC)
ContentAcademic English (lectures, study texts)Everyday and workplace English
Question types22, including Write Essay, Re-tell Lecture and Highlight Correct Summary19, including Write Email instead of an essay
Test lengthAbout 2 hoursAbout 2 hours
Scores10–90 overall and per skill10–90 per skill, converted by IRCC to CLB levels
ResultsTypically within 48 hoursTypically within 48 hours
Can I use PTE Core for university admission?
PTE Core is designed for Canadian immigration (permanent residency and citizenship). For study, choose PTE Academic and check your university's requirements.
Is the score scale the same?
Both tests report scores from 10 to 90. For PTE Core, IRCC converts each skill score to a Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) level.
Where do I take the test?
Both tests are taken on a computer at a Pearson test centre and last about two hours. You book your test directly with Pearson.
How quickly do results arrive?
Pearson typically releases results within about 48 hours, often sooner.

Courses

How long are the courses?
Group courses run for 6 weeks. Private full courses and skill-focused private courses have a flexible duration that fits your schedule.
How big are the groups?
Group courses have 5–7 students, with weekly live classes of 1.5 hours.
Are classes online?
Yes. Classes are delivered live online, and students practise on our Student Portal between classes.
Can I focus on just one skill?
Yes. The private skill-focused course lets you choose 1, 2 or 3 skills: 75 USD for one skill, 125 USD for two and 170 USD for three. Choose your skills.
Do I get a mock exam?
Yes. Our Student Portal includes full mock exams that follow the real PTE Academic or PTE Core format and timing, and your trainer gives you a full performance review afterwards.

Payments

How can I pay?
By Visa or Mastercard on our secure payment page, or by CliQ or bank transfer. See the Payments page.
Is card payment secure?
Yes. Card payments are processed on our payment provider's secure page. PTE Decode Academy never sees or stores your card details.
What happens after I pay?
Send us your receipt on WhatsApp. We'll confirm your place, your class schedule and your personal Student Portal access code.
Do prices include the Pearson exam fee?
No. Course prices cover our training only. You book and pay for your PTE test directly with Pearson.

Student Portal

How do I get access?
Enrolled students receive a personal access code and invite link. Open it in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer with a microphone.
How does feedback work?
After answering a question, click “Submit for teacher feedback”. Your trainer reviews it and replies with a score, written comments and sometimes a voice message, shown under “My work & feedback”.
Are the automatic scores official?
No. They are estimates to guide your practice. Only Pearson issues official PTE scores.
