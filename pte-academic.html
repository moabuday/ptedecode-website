About PTE Academic

PTE Academic is a computer-based English test accepted by thousands of universities, colleges and governments around the world. It assesses your Speaking, Writing, Reading and Listening skills through real-life academic tasks.

The test takes about two hours and has three parts. Since August 2025 it includes 22 question types, including the newer Summarize Group Discussion and Respond to a Situation speaking tasks.

Frequently asked questions

Who should take PTE Academic?
Students applying to universities and colleges, professionals who need English for registration, and applicants for work or migration visas that accept PTE Academic. Always check the score your institution or visa programme requires.
How long does the test take?
About two hours, in one sitting, at a Pearson test centre. There is no scheduled break.
How is PTE Academic scored?
You receive an overall score and a score for each skill (Listening, Reading, Speaking and Writing) on a scale of 10 to 90. Many tasks count towards more than one skill.
When will I get my results?
Pearson typically releases results within about 48 hours of your test.
Which course should I choose?
The group course suits most students who want complete preparation in a small class. Choose the private full course for one-to-one coaching on your schedule, or the skill-focused course if you only need to improve one, two or three skills.
