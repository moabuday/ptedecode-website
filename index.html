Not sure which test you need?

The two exams look similar but serve different goals. Always check what your university or immigration programme requires.

PTE AcademicPTE Core
Best forUniversity and college study, professional registration, and work and migration visas in many countriesCanadian immigration: permanent residency and citizenship (accepted by IRCC)
ContentAcademic English (lectures, study texts)Everyday and workplace English
Question types22, including Write Essay, Re-tell Lecture and Highlight Correct Summary19, including Write Email instead of an essay
Test lengthAbout 2 hoursAbout 2 hours
Scores10–90 overall and per skill10–90 per skill, converted by IRCC to CLB levels
ResultsTypically within 48 hoursTypically within 48 hours
