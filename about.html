Meet your trainer

Our teaching is built on real exam experience: your trainer achieved a perfect 90 in both PTE Academic and PTE Core — 90 in Listening, Reading, Speaking and Writing — through more than 20 exam attempts and extensive strategy development.

That experience shaped a simple idea: most students don't need more English — they need to understand exactly how each question type is scored and practise the right technique until it becomes automatic.
