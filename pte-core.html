About PTE Core

PTE Core is an English test accepted by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) for Canadian immigration and permanent residency applications. It assesses your Speaking, Writing, Reading and Listening skills using everyday and workplace English.

The test takes about two hours and has 19 question types. Instead of an essay, the main writing task is Write Email. IRCC converts your scores in each skill to Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) levels.

PTE Core is designed for immigration, not university admission. If you plan to study, choose PTE Academic.

Frequently asked questions

Who should take PTE Core?
Applicants for Canadian economic immigration programmes, permanent residency and citizenship who need to prove their English. Check the CLB level your programme requires.
How is PTE Core different from PTE Academic?
PTE Core uses everyday and workplace English and has 19 question types, with Write Email instead of Write Essay. PTE Academic uses academic English and has 22 question types. PTE Core is for Canadian immigration; PTE Academic is for study and other purposes.
How are PTE Core scores used for Canada?
You receive a score from 10 to 90 for each skill. IRCC converts each skill score to a Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) level.
When will I get my results?
Pearson typically releases results within about 48 hours of your test.
Which course should I choose?
The group course suits most students who want complete preparation in a small class. Choose the private full course for one-to-one coaching on your schedule, or the skill-focused course if you only need to improve one, two or three skills.
